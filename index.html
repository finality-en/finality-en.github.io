About Finality

Finality is a survival SMP built around one idea: every fight should be worth it.

You start like any survival world: gather, build, and team up with friends. On top of that, Finality adds custom weapons, armour and tools that you can craft or win, seasonal classes that give you permanent abilities, and a daily King of the Hill event (9pm UK / 4pm US East) where the strongest players fight for the best loot on the server.

Each season is a fresh start with new gear to chase. Pick a class, gear up, claim the hill, and make a name for yourself.

Teams

Team up with up to 3 friends, which makes 4 players per team. Your team gets a shared home, locked chests and a bank, plus its own chat.

Create a team/team create <name>. Names are 2–12 letters or numbers.
Invite your friends/team invite <player>. They have 2 minutes to accept with /team join <team>.
Set a home/team sethome where you live, then anyone on the team can use /team home.

What your team gets

🛡 No friendly fire

You can't hurt your teammates, and your harmful potions don't affect them. You also don't bump into each other.

💬 Team chat

Start a message with ! to send it to your team only, or use /team chat to switch to team chat.

🏠 Home & warp

/team home takes you to your base after 3 seconds, and you must stand still. Your team also gets 1 warp: /team warp.

⚓ Respawn anchor

/team anchor makes you respawn at your team home when you die.

📦 Team chests

Lock up to 2 chests so only your team can open them (/team chest claim).

💰 Team bank

Pool money with /team deposit <amount>. Check it with /team bal. Only admins and owners can withdraw.

🏆 Team score

Every player kill earns your team 1 point. See the leaderboard with /team top.

🏷 Name & tag

Your team name shows in front of your name. Owners can set a tag (/team tag) and a colour (/team color).

✚ Healer class

The Healer's aura heals the people on your team, so teaming up with a Healer pays off. Classes guide →

🤝

No alliances. Teams can't ally with each other, so anyone outside your team is fair game.

PvP & combat

PvP is on everywhere. Here's what you need to know before your first fight.

⚔ Combat tag: 20 secondsHitting or getting hit by a player puts you in combat for 20 seconds. Your action bar shows your enemy, their health and the timer. Killing your enemy ends it straight away. Throwing an ender pearl or wind charge resets the timer.
☠ Don't log out in combatLeaving while tagged kills you and drops all your items and XP, and the whole server is told you tried to escape. Getting kicked counts too.
🧱 No running to safe zonesWhile you're tagged, a red glass wall stops you entering safe zones, and you can still be hit inside one.

While you're in combat

❌ Blocked✅ Still allowed
Teleporting (/home, /tpa, /spawn…)Ender pearls (15s cooldown in combat)
All commands except /msg, /tell and /combattagEating, golden apples and totems
Riptide tridentsChorus fruit
Elytra gliding (you drop if you're gliding when tagged)Placing and breaking blocks
FlyingYour inventory and portals

Your inventory and chat close the moment you're tagged. Harmful splash potions (Slowness, Poison, Weakness…) tag you too.

🗿

Totem limit: 2. You can carry at most 2 Totems of Undying (inventory, hotbar and offhand together). Extra totems drop on the ground, and you can't pick up more once you're at the limit. Totems also can't be stored in bundles, shulker boxes or ender chests. Any you put there are moved back to you.

Good to know

🛡 New player grace

New players are protected from PvP for their first 30 minutes, shown on a boss bar. It can't be turned off early, and /rtp is blocked until it ends.

💰 Kill rewards

Earn 25 coins per player kill (once a minute). Killing the same player more than 5 times in 42 seconds gets you kicked for kill abuse.

⏱ After you die

You respawn instantly with 6 seconds of protection. After a PvP death, /back is locked for 5 seconds.

❤ Health bars

Everyone's health shows under their name, and beside it while they're in combat.

🎆 Elytra rockets

Rockets have a 2-second cooldown while you're in combat.

Anti-cheat & fair play

Finality runs a strong anti-cheat and an anti-x-ray system. Cheaters get caught, and they don't get many chances.

🛡 Anti-cheat

Movement, combat and building are checked all the time. Fly, speed, reach, kill aura, auto-clickers, scaffold and the rest all get flagged automatically, and staff review the logs.

💎 Anti-x-ray

Ores stay hidden until you can actually see them, so x-ray mods and texture packs show you nothing. Suspicious mining is logged too.

🗺 Minimaps

Minimaps are fine, but cave mode and player/entity radar are switched off on the server.

Punishments

These apply to hacked clients, x-ray, macros, exploits and any other unfair advantage. Offences count across the whole server and don't reset between seasons.

OffencePunishment
1st offence7-day ban, and anything gained by cheating is wiped
2nd offence30-day ban, and your inventory, ender chest and anything gained by cheating are wiped
3rd offencePermanent ban
⚠

Ban evasion means a permanent ban straight away. Joining on an alt while banned bans every account involved. If you think a ban was a mistake, appeal in our Discord.
