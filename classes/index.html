Finality Classes Guide

Choose your classes and earn permanent abilities for the whole season. This guide covers how to pick, unlock and switch classes, and what each one does.

Permanent abilities 1 default + 1 special Unlockable classes Daily KOTH rolls

Getting started

It takes about a minute.

Join the serverA small menu opens on its own the first time you join, showing the 4 default classes. It stays open until you pick one, and you can't be hurt while it's open. Special classes show up the next time you type /class.
Pick a default classLeft-click one of the default classes. Its ability switches on straight away and lasts the whole season, even through deaths and milk.
Unlock a special classSpecial classes sit on top of your default class, so you get both abilities. Win the daily KOTH event to get a Special Class Roll, spin it, and equip whatever you unlock from the same menu.

Your class slots

The menu title shows how many classes you have, for example (1/2) or (2/2).

🔥 Default class slot 1
⚡ Special class slot 2
➕ Second default with an Extra Class Token

Everyone starts with 2 slots: one default class and one special class. An Extra Class Token adds a third slot for a second default class, so your count becomes /3.

🏷

Every default class has 2 levels. Upgrade yours to Level II with a Class Upgrade Token. The /class menu shows your level, an XP bar, and ✔ Ready when you can upgrade.

⬆ Class upgrades

Use a Class Upgrade Token to take a default class from Level I to Level II.

Craft a tokenPut a Nether Star in the middle, Diamond Blocks on the sides and Netherite Ingots in the corners (see the recipe). Every token rolls its own success chance from 50% to 100% when it's crafted, and it's written on the item.
Save up 35,000 XPThat's about level 105. The menu shows an XP bar so you can see how close you are.
Right-click the tokenPick any of the 4 default classes to upgrade, even one you're not using right now. If it works, the XP is taken and your class becomes Level II.
🎲

Upgrades can fail. If it fails, the token shatters, but you keep your XP. A higher-percentage token is worth more!

📅

Upgrades last the whole season. You keep them if you swap classes with a Reset Token and swap back. They reset when a new season starts.

Special classes

Special classes glow in the menu and sit on top of your default class. You can have one at a time.

🔒

Special classes start LOCKED. Unlock them with a Special Class Roll from winning KOTH. Once unlocked they show UNLOCKED and you can equip them.

🪓

Berserker ⚒︎

Special

Fewer hearts, harder hits. Every kill makes you tougher.

  • Start with 7 hearts
  • +½ heart per player kill (max 15 hearts)
  • Bonus hearts reset when you die
  • +10% damage
Common · 🔒 unlock with a Class Roll
🗡️

Knight ⚔︎

Special

A sword master who shrugs off hits and makes enemies bleed.

  • +20% sword damage
  • 25% less damage from maces
  • 10% less damage from other melee weapons
  • 15% chance per sword hit to cause bleeding
  • Bleeding: 1 heart every 5s + brief slowness
  • 2% chance of a 30s deep wound
Rare · 🔒 unlock with a Class Roll
✚

Healer ✚︎

Special

Heals your team. Sneak to heal fast and repair your armour.

  • Permanent Regeneration I
  • Heals teammates within 6 blocks every 10s
  • Sneak: heal 7.5x faster and repair your armour
  • Teammates nearby heal and repair at half
  • While active: Slowness IV, 75% less damage dealt
  • Lasts 10s in total (or until healed and repaired)
  • 5 minute cooldown after that
Epic · 🔒 unlock with a Class Roll
☠️

Assassin ☠︎

Special

After a kill you are completely invisible, armour and weapon too.

  • +20% melee damage when hitting from behind
  • On kill: completely invisible for 3s
  • Your armour and weapon are hidden too
  • On kill: Speed III for 10s
  • On kill: removes bad effects, fire and bleeding (40s cooldown)
Legendary · 🔒 unlock with a Class Roll
⚡

Thor ⚡︎

Special

Every 5th hit calls down lightning on your enemy.

  • Every 5th hit (sword, axe, mace or bow): lightning
  • Lightning deals 2 hearts true damage
  • A mace smash calls it instantly
  • 30s cooldown between strikes
Epic · 🔒 unlock with a Class Roll
🩸

Bloodlust ❤︎

Special

Chain hits for huge damage, but you lose ½ heart every chain hit.

  • Per kill: +30% damage, +5% attack speed (x5, 30s)
  • Hit 3 times in a row to start a chain
  • Each chain hit: +9% damage, +3% attack speed
  • Each chain hit costs you ½ heart (1 heart from chain 7)
  • Chains end after 3s without a hit
Legendary · 🔒 unlock with a Class Roll

🎰 KOTH Special Class Roll

Win the daily KOTH event to get a Special Class Roll. Spin it for a 50/50 shot at a special class, or some trash.

Win KOTHThe winner of each daily KOTH gets a Special Class Roll (a glowing nether star).
Right-click itA roulette spins through special classes and junk, slows down, and lands on one. Half of all rolls land on trash like a Wet Stick or Pocket Lint, which you keep as a souvenir.
Win a class for the seasonIf you land on a class, it's unlocked until the season ends. The whole server and the Discord see what you rolled. Equip it from /class.

Switching special classes

Switching special classes is free. You don't need a token.

You click…What happens
A special class, with none equippedIt's equipped on top of your default class.
A different special classYou switch to it.
The special you already haveIt's unequipped.
⏱️

There's a short 5 second cooldown between changes.

Tokens

Special items for your classes. Right-click to use them.

🟠

Class Reset Token

Item

Right-click, then confirm, to reset your default class and pick a new one straight away.

  • Your special class is kept
  • A green button confirms, a red one goes back
  • Uses one token
💜

Extra Class Token

Item

Right-click to add a second default class, so you could have Pharo, Ninja and a special at once.

  • Only default classes can be picked
  • It can't be one you already have
  • Your slots become /3
  • Get one from KOTH or crates
💠

Class Upgrade Token

Legendary

Right-click to upgrade one of your default classes to Level II.

  • Costs 35,000 XP (about level 105) plus the token
  • Each token has its own success chance (50–100%)
  • Crafted, see Class upgrades

FAQ

Do I lose my ability when I die?

No. Class abilities come back automatically after death, milk or effect clears. The only exception is Berserker's bonus hearts, which reset on death.

Can I have two special classes?

No, you can only have one special class at a time. You can switch between special classes whenever you like, with a 5 second cooldown.

Why didn't my kill count for Berserker or Bloodlust?

Only real player kills count. Berserker uses the server's PvP stats, so kills the anti-farming system rejects don't give hearts.

I picked the wrong default class. What now?

Use a Class Reset Token. It resets your default class and keeps your special class.

How do I unlock special classes?

Win the daily KOTH event. The winner gets a Special Class Roll, which has a 50% chance to unlock a random special class for the rest of the season.

Why did I get a Wet Stick?

Half of all Class Rolls land on trash. You keep the junk as a souvenir, and the roll is used up. Better luck at the next KOTH!

What if I roll a class I already have?

Duplicates are lost, so that roll is used up. If you already own every special class, the roll won't let you use it, so save it for next season.

Do unlocked special classes last forever?

They last for the current season. When a new season starts, everyone starts fresh.

How do I upgrade my class?

Craft a Class Upgrade Token, save up 35,000 XP (about level 105), then right-click the token and pick your class. See Class upgrades.

Do potions still work?

Yes. A stronger potion, such as Speed II on a Ninja, takes over while it lasts, and your class effect comes back when it runs out.
